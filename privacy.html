Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026

MeterKit ("the App") is developed by Leo Mbm. This policy describes how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

MeterKit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All measurements (sound levels, magnetic field, vibration, hearing tests) are processed entirely on your device.

Microphone

The App requests microphone access to measure ambient sound levels (Sound Meter) and play calibrated tones (Hearing Test). Audio is processed in real-time on-device. No audio is recorded, stored, or sent to any server.

Motion Sensors

The App accesses the accelerometer, gyroscope, and magnetometer to power the Bubble Level, Vibration Meter, Metal Detector, and Magnetometer tools. Sensor data is processed on-device and never leaves your phone.

Calibration Data

A calibration offset value is stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device and is not linked to your identity.

Purchases

In-App Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. MeterKit does not have access to your payment information. Purchase status is verified on-device.

Analytics & Tracking

MeterKit does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. No data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

MeterKit does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
